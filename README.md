# Weekly AI Discussion

A weekly AI discussion, run as a three-month journal club on AI adoption in healthcare.

**Site:** https://iressa8655.github.io/phi-lab-ai-journal-club/

## Three focuses

1. **Adoption.** What blocks AI from being used in everyday care, and what helps. Month 1.
2. **Model types and specialties.** Why different kinds of AI suit different specialties. Month 2.
3. **Musculoskeletal care.** Which AI is used for which MSK condition. Month 3.

## How the site is updated

- Discussion questions for a week go in `data/questions.json`, keyed `w1` to `w12`.
- Answers are released after each session in `data/answers.json`, keyed the same way.
- Paper links point to PubMed or the publisher. No PDFs are hosted here.

Papers are ranked by Relative Citation Ratio from [NIH iCite](https://icite.od.nih.gov/).
